Causa limena

Description

Causa limeña is an iconic Peruvian dish that can be enjoyed on any occasion. Its combination of flavors and textures makes it a fresh, delicious, and very versatile option. It's also an economical and easy recipe to prepare at home. With these simple steps and the right ingredients, you can surprise everyone with this delicious dish.

Ingredients

  • boiled and smashed yellow potato
  • blended yellow chili pepper
  • Lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to your liking
  • Boiled and shredded chicken
  • Mayonnaise
  • Avocado

Steps

  1. In a bowl, mix the boiled yellow potato with a squeeze of lemon juice, salt, pepper, and the blended yellow chili paste. This is the base of the causa, and you should obtain a smooth, well-combined dough. To achieve the perfect texture, you can knead it with your hands or use a spatula.
  2. In another bowl, mix the cooked and shredded chicken with the mayonnaise until you have a smooth, creamy mixture. This will be the filling for the causa. You can add a touch of mustard or finely chopped onion for extra flavor.
  3. Grease a baking dish with a little oil and begin layering. First, add a layer of potato, then a layer of diced avocado, and then the chicken filling. Finish with another layer of potato.
  4. Once you've assembled the layers of the causa limeña, unmold it and decorate as you like. You can add more avocado, a touch of mayonnaise, or even garnish with olives and hard-boiled egg.
